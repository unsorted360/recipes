Gulab Jamun

Description

Gulab jamun is a popular Indian sweet made from soft milk-based dough, shaped into small balls, deep-fried until golden, and soaked in warm sugar syrup flavored with cardamom.

    Ingredients
  • Milk powder
  • All-purpose flour (maida)
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom
    Steps
  1. Prepare a soft dough using milk powder, flour, and a little water.
  2. Shape the dough into small smooth balls.
  3. Deep-fry the balls until golden brown.
  4. Soak them in warm sugar syrup flavored with cardamom.
