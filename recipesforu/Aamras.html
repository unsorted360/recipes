Aamras

Description

Aamras is a simple and delicious mango dessert made by blending ripe mangoes with sugar, cardamom, and a little milk or water until smooth and creamy.

    Ingredients
  • Ripe mangoes
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom
  • Milk or water
    Steps
  1. Peel and chop the ripe mangoes.
  2. Blend the mangoes until smooth.
  3. Add sugar, cardamom, and a little milk.
  4. Blend again and serve chilled.
